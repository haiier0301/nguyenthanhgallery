8 HEARTS

OVERVIEW WORKS INFO

In 8 Hearts, TRUC-ANH unfolds a cinematic sequence of images that move between tenderness and violence, intimacy and distance. Working with saturated colour and abrupt shifts of light, the artist constructs a psychological landscape where bodies, plants and fragments of architecture appear only briefly before dissolving again into pure rhythm and hue.

The series takes its title from the heart as both a physical organ and an unstable repository of memory. Across the works, silhouettes are stretched, cropped or interrupted, as if the image itself were struggling to hold together competing emotions – desire, fear, protection, loss. Rather than illustrating a single narrative, each painting functions as a frame within an open-ended film, inviting viewers to move in close, linger, and assemble their own sequence of events.

Installed as a vertical score of images, 8 Hearts proposes painting as a time-based medium: the slow accumulation of looking replaces the instant of recognition. The exhibition continues TRUC-ANH’s ongoing interest in vulnerability and resistance, and in how private feelings might be translated into shared, luminous surfaces.

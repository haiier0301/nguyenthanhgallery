NGUYEN THANH

ABOUT SERIES

Nguyen Thanh was born on April 20, 1976, in Quảng Bình, Vietnam. His creative inspiration stems from the beauty of nature, the rhythm of human life, and the movement of society. These elements form an endless source of emotion within the artist, nurturing a continuous current of creativity that evolves with time and the social landscape.

His paintings capture life's emotional flow through various periods and transformations, reflecting his deep sensitivity to history and human experience. The emotional core of Nguyen Thanh's art is drawn from his love for family, friendship, childhood memories, and the serenity of peace.

He is particularly fond of the color green — the color of the Earth, symbolizing harmony and life. Through his works, Nguyen Thanh embarks on a journey to seek new light — the light of human existence, of all living things, and of the future of humankind. Light, often recurring throughout his paintings, symbolizes his aspiration for a better, more peaceful world.

Nguyen Thanh often expresses this belief passionately: "I love peace; I love the green of this planet." For him, it represents the essence of life — for humanity and for all beings. Each artwork speaks a unique visual language, shaped by the ever-flowing stream of time and emotion.

Nguyen Thanh's works have been warmly embraced by collectors both in Vietnam and internationally.

VU QUOC DUNG (b. 1982)

ABOUT EXHIBITIONS ARTWORKS CV

Vu Quoc Dung was born in Thanh Hoa province in 1982. He graduated from Binh Duong College of Fine Arts and later from the University of Fine Arts in Ho Chi Minh City. Currently, he serves as a lecturer at Binh Duong College of Fine Arts and is a member of the Ho Chi Minh City Graphics Club.

Dung works primarily in graphic and mixed-media disciplines, with a strong emphasis on structure, composition, and visual symbolism. His works often explore social themes and collective memory, expressed through a distinctive visual language that balances clarity and emotional depth.

He has participated in numerous national and regional exhibitions, including the Youth Exhibition (Biennale) and the National Fine Arts Exhibition. His works have been included in charity art auctions such as Operation Smile Vietnam and are held in private collections in the United States, France, the Netherlands, Switzerland, Hong Kong, Singapore, and Malaysia.

DO THANH LANG

ABOUT EXHIBITIONS ARTWORKS CV

Do Thanh Lang's practice is centered on the use of painting as a medium of reflection and expression. His work explores the boundaries between memory and imagination, creating compositions that invite viewers to reconsider their relationship with space, time, and cultural identity.

ARTWORK TITLE, 2022

Through a meticulous approach to color, texture, and form, Do Thanh Lang develops a visual language that draws from both Vietnamese artistic traditions and contemporary painting practices. His compositions often incorporate layered narratives that speak to personal and collective histories.

The artist's engagement with painting extends beyond the canvas, encompassing site-specific interventions and collaborative projects that explore the role of art in public space. His work has been presented in solo and group exhibitions across Vietnam and internationally.

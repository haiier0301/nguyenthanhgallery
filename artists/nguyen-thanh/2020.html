NGUYEN THANH

2020

THEME

"In Full Bloom"

With a rich diversity of themes and an unrelenting dedication over his 25-year artistic journey, Nguyen Thanh has continuously explored, learned, and reinvented himself, creating ever-new, distinctive bodies of work that captivate viewers and lead them from one emotional state to another. Each series, shaped by his inner reflections and quiet confessions, carries a wish, a prayer for goodness, and a longing for a gentle, peaceful life amid a fragile and fractured world.

Through these concerns and contemplations, the artist persistently sends out messages of tranquility — stories of a green world and a vision of peace for humankind. As he shares: "I, myself, here and somewhere out there in this world, simply wish to watch flowers in full bloom, sip a cup of tea, and gaze at drifting clouds. That is peace."

In the theme "In Full Bloom", he paints clusters of flowers in exuberant bloom beside windows and quiet corners, as if they were secretly hiding in silence only to release their fragrance softly into the wind. These floral forms become metaphors for inner calm — a discreet yet radiant presence that gently permeates space and spirit.

Looking ahead, the artist describes his next project as a globally resonant undertaking. His upcoming theme, "For a Peaceful and Harmonious World", is conceived as a heartfelt message to international audiences — an artistic appeal for peace, serenity, and a greener future for all.

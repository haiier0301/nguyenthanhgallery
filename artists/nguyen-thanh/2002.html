NGUYEN THANH

2002

THEME

"my hometown"

Those early years living and working in Ho Chi Minh City...

In a vibrant, bustling industrial metropolis... That hectic, fast-paced rhythm of life. Nostalgia for my homeland haunted my dreams, weaving into my daily existence. Images of my mother, rural farmers, village children, stories, and childhood memories flooded back... Countless recollections of my youth emerged, blending with the colors of my homeland to form simple, timeless narratives...

The Flowers in the City series explores the delicate intersection between organic forms and urban environment. Through expressive brushwork and layered compositions, these works capture fleeting moments of natural beauty within the concrete landscape.

Each painting in this series balances controlled structure with spontaneous gesture, allowing color and texture to convey emotional resonance rather than literal representation.

NGUYEN THANH

2016

THEME

"Green Planet"

With tireless creativity and dedication, Nguyen Thanh continually explores new narratives and emotions, renewing both his brushwork and color language across diverse themes, each one a story he shares with art lovers at home and abroad.

From earlier compositions that evoked a sense of peace, he moves into a strikingly different visual realm of tension and contrast, as if multiple emotional currents within him are waiting for the moment of release. Human figures appear to be flying around a great sphere, sometimes soaring upward, sometimes spiraling inward, with trees and natural forms drawn into the same orbit — suggesting a profound inner concern.

As the artist reflects: "Nature has given us precious and beautiful things; if humanity does not know how to use and preserve them, they may shatter. Let us protect our green planet — not only for today, but for tomorrow." The recurring image of spherical forms floating in space alludes to galaxies in constant motion, reminding viewers of a living, fragile universe that demands awareness and responsibility.

NGUYEN TRAN

ABOUT ARTWORKS CV

Nguyen Tran is a contemporary Vietnamese artist whose practice engages with the intersection of abstraction and representation. Working primarily in oil on canvas, his artistic approach emphasizes the exploration of form, color relationships, and spatial composition.

Through sustained attention to materiality and surface, Nguyen Tran develops a visual language that balances gestural spontaneity with deliberate structure. His works reflect an ongoing investigation into the expressive potential of paint itself, allowing process and medium to shape the final form.

Nguyen Tran has participated in various group exhibitions throughout Vietnam and maintains an active studio practice. His works are held in private collections domestically and internationally, reflecting a commitment to contemporary painting traditions while pursuing an individual artistic vision.

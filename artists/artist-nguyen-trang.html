NGUYEN TRANG

ABOUT ARTWORKS CV

Nguyen Trang (born 1974 in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam) is a contemporary Vietnamese artist whose practice encompasses both domestic and international exhibition contexts. Her artistic career is marked by active participation in collaborative exhibitions and humanitarian initiatives, demonstrating a sustained commitment to both artistic development and social engagement.

Working primarily in oil on canvas, Nguyen Trang has developed a distinctive visual language that balances formal aesthetic concerns with accessible imagery. Her practice reflects ongoing dialogue with contemporary painting traditions while maintaining connection to lived experience and cultural context.

Nguyen Trang has participated in charity fundraising exhibitions benefiting underprivileged children, including the notable "From the Heart" exhibition with the Ho Chi Minh City Red Cross in 2017, which raised funds for orphans and people with disabilities. Her works are held in private collections internationally, including the United Kingdom, France, Switzerland, Sweden, Singapore, South Korea, Hong Kong, and the United States, reflecting both the accessibility and resonance of her artistic vision.

Through consistent participation in group exhibitions across Vietnam and internationally, Nguyen Trang continues to develop her artistic practice, contributing to the contemporary Vietnamese art dialogue while maintaining an authentic personal vision.

SPOTLIGHT AWARD — RED DOT MIAMI ART FAIR

DECEMBER 2025 / AWARD

Nguyen Thanh is awarded the Spotlight Award at Red Dot Miami Art Fair 2025 in recognition of his signature atmospheric paintings exploring light, atmosphere, and architectural space. The presentation received strong interest from collectors and the jury alike.

PARTICIPATION AT AFFORDABLE ART FAIR LONDON

MARCH 2023 / EXHIBITION

Nguyen Thanh presented a selection of recent works at Affordable Art Fair London 2023, showcasing luminous urban landscapes and meditative interiors. The presentation marked the artist's debut with the fair and introduced his paintings to a wider international audience.

AFFORDABLE ART FAIR LONDON — SELECTED WORKS

OCTOBER 2022 / EXHIBITION

A curated group of Nguyen Thanh's paintings was highlighted in the Selected Works section of Affordable Art Fair London 2022. The works focused on twilight cityscapes and reflective waterways, emphasising the artist's subtle use of colour and light.
